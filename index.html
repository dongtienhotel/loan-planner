What's inside

Standard amortization

Monthly amortization for every loan: payment, interest, principal and balance per period.

My Loans tracker

Follow real loans to payoff with progress, remaining balance and the next payment date.

Payment reminders

Notifications scheduled on your device before each due date.

Debt Priority

Rank several loans by Avalanche (highest rate first) or Snowball (lowest balance first).

Compare and convert

Compare loan options, plus temperature, mass, speed and length converters, an approximate offline currency converter and a world clock.

No account

No sign-up. Your loan inputs, tracked loans and saved calculations stay on your device.

How it works

Calculate, track and stay on top of it.

Calculate

Pick a loan type, enter the amount, rate, term and start date, and get the payment and the amortization schedule.

Track this loan

Tap once to save it as a tracked loan. Progress and the next payment date follow from your start date.

Stay ahead

Turn on a reminder, mark periods paid and use Debt Priority when you are juggling more than one loan.

FAQ
Is Loan Planner free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, and it may show ads. Every calculator and the loan tracker are available without paying.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your calculations and tracked loans are stored on your device.

Is this financial advice?

No. Results are estimates based on the figures you enter, using standard monthly amortization. Actual loan terms, fees and rounding may differ by lender. Please confirm the details with your lender.

Does the currency converter use live rates?

No. The currency converter uses approximate built-in rates and works offline, so treat it as indicative only.

What data do you collect?

Your loan figures stay on your device. The app uses analytics, crash reporting and advertising or attribution services, described in full in the privacy policy.
