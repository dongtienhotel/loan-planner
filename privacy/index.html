Privacy Policy

Loan Planner · Effective date: October 5, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data Loan Planner ("the App", "we", "us") handles, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match what the App actually does. Loan Planner is a loan and savings calculator with an optional loan-tracking feature: Personal, Business, Mortgage and Auto loan calculators, Fixed Deposit and Recurring Deposit calculators, amortization schedules, a loan-comparison tool, unit and currency converters, and a "My Loans" tracker with payment reminders and a Debt Priority view.

2. The financial numbers you enter stay on your device

Loan amounts, interest rates, terms, start dates, tracked-loan progress, reminder settings, and any other figures you type into a calculator or into "My Loans" are stored only on your device, in local app storage. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or can read these numbers. Deleting a loan in the App, or uninstalling the App, removes this data. The App has no account and no sign-in, so this data is never linked to your identity on our side.

3. Data collected by third-party services

The App is free and supported by advertising. It includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) that run inside the App and may collect limited technical data from your device. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any account credential, because the App never asks for them.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob, with mediation partners AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle and Liftoff/Vungle): to request, show and measure ads, these services may collect your Android Advertising ID, device and software information (such as device model, OS version, language, and approximate region derived from your IP address), and ad-interaction events (impressions, clicks).
  • Attribution and ad measurement (Adjust, AppsFlyer, TikTok Business SDK, Meta SDK): these services may collect your Advertising ID, install and referrer information and ad-campaign events so that advertisers and we can tell which campaigns led to installs and how ads perform.
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): app-interaction data (for example which screens and features are used), device information and app instance identifiers, used to understand and improve the App.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): if the App crashes or hits an error, crash logs and device/OS diagnostics are sent so we can fix the problem.
  • Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config): the App downloads configuration values (for example ad and feature settings) from Firebase. Your loan and calculator data is not part of this.
  • Consent (Google User Messaging Platform): where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App shows a consent form before personalised advertising is used, and records your choice.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy. Data collected by them in connection with the App is also sent to and processed on their servers, which may be located outside your country.

4. Permissions the App requests

  • Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state: required for ads, analytics, crash reports and remote configuration.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android Ad Services permissions: let the advertising and attribution services above read your Advertising ID and use Android's privacy-preserving attribution. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google Settings → Ads).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used for the optional payment reminders described below. You can decline or revoke it at any time.

5. Payment reminders (notifications)

If you turn on a reminder for a tracked loan, the App uses Android's WorkManager to schedule a notification on your own device a number of days before the loan's next payment date. The reminder is created and shown entirely on-device; your loan details are not sent anywhere to produce it.

6. Sharing results as PDF

If you choose "Share PDF", the App creates a PDF of the calculation on your device and hands it to the share sheet. It is only sent to whichever app or contact you pick; we do not receive a copy.

7. Purchases

The App checks with Google Play whether an ad-free entitlement is associated with your Google account. Any payment would be handled by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The only sharing is the technical data described in section 3, which the service providers listed there receive in order to deliver advertising, attribution, analytics and crash reporting for the App. We may also disclose information if required by law.

9. Retention and deletion

Your loan and calculator data lives only on your device and is deleted when you delete the loan in the App or uninstall the App. For the technical data held by third-party providers (advertising identifiers, analytics, crash diagnostics), retention is governed by those providers' policies. You can:

  • reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings;
  • withdraw or change your consent choice where the consent form is shown; and
  • email us at dongtienhotel@gmail.com to ask about data associated with your device identifiers; we will follow up with our service providers as applicable.

Because the App has no account system, there is no account to delete.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no identifiable account data, most requests concern identifiers processed by the service providers in section 3. Contact us at dongtienhotel@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Children's privacy

Loan Planner is a personal-finance planning tool for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will work with our providers to address it.

12. Security

Network communication used by the App's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) wherever the provider supports it. Your loan data stays in app-private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
